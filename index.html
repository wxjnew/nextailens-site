PictureLens

A powerful, privacy-first photo metadata viewer for iOS and Android. Explore every detail your camera records.

File Information

File name, type, size, dimensions, DPI, color space, and creation date at a glance.

Shooting Parameters

Aperture, shutter speed, ISO, focal length, metering mode, and more from your camera settings.

GPS & Map

View photo location on an interactive map with reverse geocoding for place names.

RAW Maker Data

Brand-specific metadata from Nikon, Canon, Sony, Fuji, and other camera manufacturers.

Thumbnail Preview

Extract and view the embedded thumbnail image stored inside your photo file.

Export & Share

Export metadata as plain text or JSON and share via the system share sheet.

Key Features

  • Supports JPEG, TIFF, HEIC, PNG, AVIF, WebP, and RAW formats (CR2, NEF, ARW, RAF, etc.)
  • 7 organized tabs: File Info, Main Image, Photography, RAW Maker, GPS, Thumbnail, Interoperability
  • Batch viewing — open up to 20 photos at once
  • All processing happens locally on your device — no uploads, no cloud
  • Dark mode and light mode support
  • English and Chinese localization

Available On

iOS and Android
